02 / The Philosophy

Why most video edits fail on social.

Most editors treat social videos like traditional film: slow introductions, heavy branding upfront, and static transitions. Social algorithms punish that immediately.

Retention-First Architecture

"I don't just make videos look pretty. I think about what makes someone stop scrolling, understand the message within 3 seconds, and stay through the call to action."

Pacing Over Flash

Cutting dead air, breaths, and micro-pauses while keeping the speaker feeling natural and energetic.

Intentional Visual Hooks

Overlaying relevant b-roll, motion graphics, and UI cues that reinforce what's spoken, not random stock clips.

Sound Design That Drives Rhythm

Subtle whooshes, risers, and tactile clicks that emphasize key value points without overpowering voice audio.

Native Platform Polish

Safe zones tailored for TikTok, IG Reels, and Shorts so captions and elements are never blocked by platform UI.
